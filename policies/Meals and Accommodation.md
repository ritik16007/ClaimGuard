# Meals and Accommodation — fictional MVP policy

> Fictional sample policy for the ClaimGuard demonstration. INR throughout.

## Page 1: Business meals

Reasonable meals during approved business travel are eligible. A meal claim up to and including INR 1,500 per person per meal can be auto-approved if evidence and all other checks pass. A larger amount is held for authority review.

## Page 2: Meal documentation

Attach an itemised receipt and state the business context. For a hosted meal, list attendees and their organisation. Alcohol, personal groceries, minibar snacks, and meals unrelated to business are not reimbursable.

## Page 3: Accommodation limit

A standard room for approved travel is eligible up to INR 5,000 per night. A claim at or below that amount may be auto-approved if it passes every check. Higher nightly cost needs authority review and an explanation; no automatic exception is granted.

## Page 4: Hotel booking and evidence

Choose a standard room and cancel unused bookings promptly. Submit the hotel folio showing stay dates, nightly rate, taxes, and total. A receipt date that conflicts with the stay dates is held for review.

## Page 5: Combined bills and taxes

When a single bill includes several categories, split the amounts into separate claims if possible. If the bill cannot be separated, submit it as an unknown or mixed category for authority review. Applicable taxes within an eligible item are included in its limit.

## Page 6: Meals and lodging exceptions

A manager may review a documented exception due to safety, accessibility, availability, or a client requirement. Attach the reason and prior approval if available. An exception is a request, not an automatic entitlement.
