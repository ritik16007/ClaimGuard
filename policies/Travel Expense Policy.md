# Travel Expense Policy — fictional MVP policy

> Fictional sample policy for the ClaimGuard demonstration. INR throughout.

## Page 1: Purpose and scope

This fictional handbook applies to employees on approved business travel. It is a product demonstration, not a real employer policy. Claims are denominated in Indian rupees (INR). Only necessary, reasonable business costs with a receipt may be reimbursed.

## Page 2: Before you travel

Obtain manager approval for the trip before booking. Record the business purpose, destination, dates, and project. Choose reasonably priced options. Personal extensions and companion costs are not reimbursable.

## Page 3: Local transport

Taxi, metro, bus, and business parking are eligible when used for a work journey. Include the route or purpose. A single local-travel claim up to and including INR 5,000 may be auto-approved when all evidence checks pass. Above INR 5,000 requires authority review.

## Page 4: Air and rail

Use standard economy air or second-class/AC rail where practical. Premium cabins, upgrades, missed-ticket fees, and personal changes require prior written exception approval. Attach the itinerary and proof of payment.

## Page 5: Mileage and private vehicles

A private vehicle may be used when practical public transport is unavailable or a manager approves it. State origin, destination, and business purpose. Fuel and mileage for the same journey cannot both be claimed.

## Page 6: Rental vehicles

Choose the smallest suitable vehicle and return it on time. Fuel, tolls, and parking require itemised receipts. Fines, damage caused by negligence, upgrades, and personal-use days are excluded.

## Page 7: Submission deadline

Submit within 30 calendar days after the expense date; day 30 is included and day 31 is late. Weekends and holidays count. Late claims are held for authority review; explain the delay.

## Page 8: Travel evidence and review

A receipt must show merchant, transaction date, amount, and currency when available. The employee-entered expense date must match the receipt date. Unreadable or conflicting evidence is sent to review, never treated as verified. Duplicate receipt files are rejected.
